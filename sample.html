A quiet preview

Five pages for where you are now.

These are not a test and there is nothing to complete. Read the page that feels closest, answer only what comes, and stop when it feels enough.

Before you begin

Come back to the room.

Let your eyes find five ordinary things. Feel where your body is supported. Exhale a little longer than you inhale. Nothing needs solving for the next sixty seconds.

What is true right now, before you make it more acceptable?

One word counts.

I’m spiralling

Separate now from next.

What is happening in this exact moment—and what is your mind predicting will happen later?

You are not being asked to prove the fear wrong. Only to notice the difference between the present and the prediction.

I feel numb

Notice the smallest signal.

Without forcing a feeling, what can you notice: pressure, temperature, tiredness, distance, heaviness, nothing at all?

“Nothing” is still information.

Something’s moving

Let the change speak quietly.

What are you beginning to know that you have not yet been ready to say aloud?

You do not need to act on it today.

I’m ready to let go

Release one job.

What are you still asking yourself to carry, fix, explain or earn that may no longer be yours?

Letting go can begin as permission, not a dramatic ending.

The full journal

Open where you are. Leave when you have had enough.

Sit With It contains four emotional entry points, grounding cues, original prompts and space for what has not become a sentence yet.
